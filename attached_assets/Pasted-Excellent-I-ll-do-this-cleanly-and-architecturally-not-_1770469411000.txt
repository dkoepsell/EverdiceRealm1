# PART II — CAML AUTHORING CHECKLIST FOR DMs

This is the thing you can laminate, ship, or encode.

---

## CAML DM AUTHORING CHECKLIST (PRE-FLIGHT)

### 1. Campaign Spine

* [ ] Can you state the campaign question in **one sentence**?
* [ ] Does every chapter move that question forward?

If not, cut content.

---

### 2. Stakes

* [ ] Are there **2–4 stakes only**?
* [ ] Can each go up *and* down?
* [ ] Does every major action touch at least one stake?

If an action touches none, it is flavor only.

---

### 3. Choices

* [ ] Does every choice **cost**, **close**, or **escalate**?
* [ ] Can any choice be repeated safely?

  * If yes, redesign or remove it.

“No cost” = “no choice”.

---

### 4. Combat

* [ ] What stake caused this combat?
* [ ] What gets worse even if the party wins?
* [ ] Can the party avoid this fight by making a different commitment earlier?

Combat should be consequence, not content.

---

### 5. Chapters

* [ ] What belief changes in this chapter?
* [ ] What truth becomes irreversible?
* [ ] Why does the chapter end *now*?

Never advance chapters due to time spent.

---

### 6. Rewards

* [ ] Is XP awarded per **resolution**, not per action?
* [ ] Do items change future choices, not just numbers?
* [ ] Does power increase pressure somewhere else?

Power without cost breaks tension.

---

### 7. Logging & CAML Trace

* [ ] Did you log *why* a chapter advanced?
* [ ] Did you log *why* XP was awarded?
* [ ] Are foreclosed options explicitly recorded?

Future-you is a user too.
