The Core Issue

Your hero section is good.

But it is still slightly abstract:

Play D&D without the friction.

That is clear.
But it is not vivid.

Try tightening it to something more concrete:

Play D&D without scheduling stress, prep overload, or lost story threads.

That names the pain directly.

The Best Line on Your Page

This is the line that matters:

It's a memory engine for your campaign.

That is your differentiator.

You are not an AI storyteller.
You are not a VTT.
You are not a rules compendium.

You are persistence.

That needs to be elevated visually.

Make it its own block:

Everdice is a memory engine for your campaign.

It tracks consequences so you don’t have to.

That should anchor the entire page.

The Section Order Is Slightly Off

Right now it flows like this:

Hero
Why campaigns fade
How you can play
What it does
Persistence
DM section
Discord
Audience breakdown

That’s too much vertical scrolling before clarity locks in.

I would restructure like this:

Social proof (even if small)

Everything else below fold

Right now, your strongest differentiator is buried in the middle.

“33 adventurers have joined”

This line is neutral.

It doesn’t help much.

If the number is small, it does not build confidence.

You could instead say:

Early beta. Feedback shapes the system.

That reframes small numbers as intentional.

The Biggest Missing Piece

There is still no immediate action path.

You say:

Start Your Adventure
Try a quick adventure right now.

That’s good.

But it needs clarity.

What exactly happens when I click?

Add a subline:

No signup required. 2-minute intro quest.

That specificity increases clicks dramatically.

The Emotional Gap

Right now the page is rational.

But D&D is emotional.

You might consider one small imaginative hook near the top:

Open a door. Roll the dice. See what happens next.

Just one sentence that feels like play.

Not marketing.
Not explanation.
Just immersion.
