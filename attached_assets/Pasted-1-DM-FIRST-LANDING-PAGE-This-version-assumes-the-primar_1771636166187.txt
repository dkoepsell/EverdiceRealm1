2️⃣ IDEAL 2-MINUTE DEMO FLOW

This is critical.

The demo must not explain.

It must demonstrate persistence.

Goal:

Within 120 seconds, the user experiences:

A choice

A consequence

A visible memory

Step 0: No Character Builder

Do NOT start with:

Class selection

Ability scores

Long forms

Preload a simple character:

You are Alric, a wandering ranger.

Give them one sentence of context.

Step 1: Immediate Scene

You arrive at a village where the mill has burned down.
The villagers whisper about goblins in the woods.

Give 3 choices:

A. Investigate the woods
B. Question the villagers
C. Ignore it and leave

No stats shown yet.
Just play.

Step 2: A Mechanical Moment

If they choose woods:

Small encounter.

Roll one attack.

Show initiative tracking automatically.

Show HP updating automatically.

This is where they feel:

“Oh. It’s handling the bookkeeping.”

Step 3: Visible Persistence

After resolution, show a subtle sidebar:

Campaign State Updated:

✔ Goblin scout defeated
✔ Village trust increased
✔ Rumor of goblin cave unlocked

That is the hook.

They see memory.

Step 4: Close With Tease

This world will remember what you did.
Want to save this campaign?

Now ask for signup.

Not before.

What the demo must NOT do:

Long exposition

Explain rules

Ask for setup choices

Talk about philosophy

Show too many buttons

It should feel like:

Door → Roll → Consequence → Memory.

3️⃣ PSYCHOLOGICAL DIAGNOSIS OF “START YOUR ADVENTURE”

Right now, here’s likely what happens internally:

Step 1: Curiosity

“Okay, let’s see what this is.”

Step 2: Cognitive Load

“Whoa, there’s a lot of UI.”
“Do I need to configure something?”
“What mode am I in?”

Step 3: Ambiguity

“Am I playing solo?”
“Is this AI?”
“Is this like a VTT?”
“What am I supposed to do first?”

Step 4: Emotional Flatness

Nothing meaningful happens in first 90 seconds.

No attachment.
No tension.
No consequence.

They leave.

Retention failure rarely happens because the product is bad.

It happens because the first emotional spike comes too late.

You need:

Choice within 30 seconds.
Roll within 60 seconds.
Visible state change within 120 seconds.
