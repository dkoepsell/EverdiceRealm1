Why Everdice Exists

Most people don’t stop playing D&D because they don’t love it.
They stop because schedules clash, tables drift, or getting started feels harder than it should.

Everdice is built to remove those barriers.

You can play solo, learn the game at your own pace, bring friends in later, or step into the DM role when you’re ready. The system supports you without taking over.

Built for Real Tables, Not Replacements

Everdice isn’t a virtual tabletop and doesn’t try to be one.

You roll physical dice. You talk freely. You improvise.
Everdice quietly handles continuity, notes, characters, and campaign memory so you can focus on roleplaying.

Campaigns remember what happened. Characters persist. Stories don’t vanish between sessions.

Works Where Your Group Already Is

If your group uses Discord, Everdice can meet you there.

Build your campaign in Everdice, then deploy it to your Discord server for sessions, coordination, and continuity. Chat and voice stay in Discord. Everdice keeps the campaign coherent in the background.

No lock-in. No forced workflow changes.
